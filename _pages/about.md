<p style="margin-top: 2em;">
I am currently affiliated with the School of Automation at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Macau under the supervision of Prof. Chi-Man Pun. As part of my joint Ph.D. training, I spent one year at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University under the supervision of Prof. Kin-Man Lam.
</p>

My early research began with human facial attribute analysis, which taught me that robust representation learning is the core of machine perception. However, I soon hit an implicit bottleneck: purely discriminative approaches limit representation quality. Understanding and generation are two sides of the same coin. Just as a creator understands a painting far better than a critic, a model's ability to generate data implies a deeper understanding of it. To overcome this bottleneck, I pivoted to exploring representation learning through a generative lens, aligning with Richard Feynman’s principle: *"What I cannot create, I do not understand."*


Building on this generative perspective, my current applied research focuses on human-centric dynamic world modeling and simulation. This framework centers on humans and their surrounding environments, driven by three fundamental processes: reconstruction, inference, and interaction. I hope that advancing these technical frontiers will empower us with greater freedom, both materially and spiritually.

<p style="margin-top: 2em;">
<strong>Prospective master's and undergraduate students</strong>: If you are interested in joining my research group, please email me with your CV.
</p>
